## 1. Research Governance & Verification Standards

In compliance with KisanVision operating rules, no agricultural claim or diagnostic class is deployed to production without an authoritative classification:
1. `VERIFIED_FROM_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE`: Backed by peer-reviewed literature, university package of practices (PJTSAU), or national agricultural research institutes (ICAR).
2. `CANDIDATE_REQUIRES_EXPERT_REVIEW`: Documented in regional extension notes or emerging reports (e.g., recent invasive pest patterns) requiring validation by a designated university entomologist or pathologist.
3. `NOT_VERIFIED`: Unconfirmed field observation or hearsay lacking formal institutional citations.

## 2. Crop 1: Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum* - ప్రత్తి / కపాస్)

### 2.1 Growth Stages & Phenology (Telangana Kharif Rainfed/Irrigated)
- **Stage 1: Germination & Emergence (0–12 DAS):** Radicle emergence, cotyledon unfolding. Susceptible to damping-off and soil moisture stress.
- **Stage 2: Seedling Stage (12–35 DAS):** First true leaf to 6-8 node formation. High susceptibility to sucking pests (thrips, aphids, jassids).
- **Stage 3: Squaring / Square Formation (35–60 DAS):** Appearance of pinhead squares and reproductive branching (sympodia).
- **Stage 4: Flowering & Peak Boll Formation (60–105 DAS):** Peak water and nutrient demand; vulnerable to bollworm entry, grey mildew, and bacterial blight.
- **Stage 5: Boll Maturation & Bursting (105–150 DAS):** Fibre elongation, boll wall hardening, and bursting. Vulnerable to pink bollworm inside bolls and internal locule rot.
- **Stage 6: Picking / Harvesting (150–180 DAS):** Multi-pass manual picking; trash and moisture management.

### 2.3 Nutrient Deficiency & Environmental Stress (Cotton)
- **Potassium (K) Deficiency vs. Leaf Reddening:**
  - *Symptom:* Marginal and interveinal chlorosis starting on older leaves, developing into necrotic bronze or red patches. Leaves become brittle.
  - *Verification:* `VERIFIED_FROM_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE` (ICAR-CICR Soil & Plant Health Manual).
  - *Visual Confusion Risk:* Often confused with *Jassid hopper burn* or physiological leaf reddening caused by low night temperatures combined with waterlogging in black soils.
- **Magnesium (Mg) Deficiency:**
  - *Symptom:* Interveinal purplish-red discoloration on lower leaves while main veins remain distinctly green.
  - *Verification:* `VERIFIED_FROM_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE` (PJTSAU Soil Science Division).

## 3. Crop 2: Rice (*Oryza sativa* - వరి)

### 3.1 Growth Stages & Phenology (Telangana Kharif & Rabi)
- **Stage 1: Nursery / Seedling (0–25 DAS):** Nursery bed establishment, radicle and shoot growth, 3-4 leaf stage prior to uprooting.
- **Stage 2: Tillering Stage (25–50 DAS):** Vegetative tillering, root proliferation, canopy closure. High susceptibility to stem borer dead hearts and blast.
- **Stage 3: Stem Elongation & Panicle Initiation (50–75 DAS):** Internode lengthening, young panicle formation inside sheath.
- **Stage 4: Booting & Heading / Flowering (75–95 DAS):** Swelling of flag leaf sheath (booting), panicle exertion, anthesis and pollination. Susceptible to neck blast and sheath blight.
- **Stage 5: Milk to Dough Stage (95–115 DAS):** Starch accumulation in caryopsis; vulnerable to false smut and stink bug feeding.
- **Stage 6: Maturity & Ripening (115–135 DAS):** Golden yellow panicles, moisture reduction (14–17% target).

### 3.3 Nutrient Deficiency & Environmental Stress (Rice)
- **Zinc Deficiency ("Khaira" Disease):**
  - *Symptom:* Uneven plant growth, chlorosis at the base of youngest leaves followed by reddish-brown or bronze pigmentation across leaf blades 2-3 weeks after transplanting in submerged alkaline soils.
  - *Verification:* `VERIFIED_FROM_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE` (ICAR-IIRR Soil Chemistry Division).
  - *Visual Confusion Risk:* Often misdiagnosed as *Brown Spot* or *Tungro virus*.
- **Submergence / Flash Flood Damage:**
  - *Symptom:* Pale, elongated, fragile leaves with mud deposition, yellowing due to light deprivation.

## 4. Crop 3: Chilli (*Capsicum annuum* - మిరప)

### 4.1 Growth Stages & Phenology (Telangana Kharif & Post-Rainy)
- **Stage 1: Nursery Stage (0–35 DAS):** Seedling beds or pro-trays; vulnerable to damping-off.
- **Stage 2: Vegetative Establishment (35–65 DAS):** Post-transplantation field establishment, lateral branching. High vulnerability to sucking pests.
- **Stage 3: Flowering & Early Fruit Set (65–95 DAS):** Profuse white flowering, fruit elongation. Critical threshold for thrips infestation and anthracnose twig die-back.
- **Stage 4: Fruit Development & Green Picking (95–130 DAS):** Green fruit maturation, multiple harvests.
- **Stage 5: Red Ripe Pod Maturation & Drying (130–180 DAS):** Pods turn deep crimson red; sun drying on drying floors (*kallam*).

## 5. Diagnostic Questions & Verification Protocols

When an image is submitted, KisanVision must dynamically prompt the user with targeted contextual verification questions to confirm or disambiguate symptoms:

### 5.1 Cotton Diagnostic Protocol
1. *Is leaf reddening uniform across the plot, or confined to isolated patches?* (Uniform = Cold/Nutrient stress; Isolated = Jassid attack).
2. *Are leaves curling upwards or downwards?* (Upwards with yellow margins = Jassid hopperburn).
3. *Break open 5 green bolls at random: Are there pink-banded larvae or brown frass inside?* (Confirms Pink Bollworm presence even before external leaf damage is visible).

### 5.2 Rice Diagnostic Protocol
1. *Are drying patches circular in shape across the field, with tillers lodged at the center?* (Confirms Brown Planthopper hopper burn).
2. *Can the affected panicle be pulled out effortlessly from the sheath base, revealing a chewed/rotted base?* (Confirms Yellow Stem Borer dead heart/white ear).
3. *Do leaf lesions exhibit a spindle shape with a whitish center and brown border?* (Distinguishes Rice Blast from Bacterial Blight).

### 5.3 Chilli Diagnostic Protocol
1. *Are leaf margins curled upwards (cup-shaped) or downwards (inverted boat)?* (Upward = Thrips; Downward = Mites).
2. *Are flowers dropping prematurely with black insects visible inside petals?* (Confirms *Thrips parvispinus* activity).
3. *Are circular sunken spots visible on red ripe pods with concentric black rings?* (Confirms Anthracnose fruit rot).
