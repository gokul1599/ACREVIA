## 1. Localization Philosophy for Risk & Expert Consultation

When a smallholder farmer in Telangana receives an agrometeorological risk alert or a certified agronomist's treatment recommendation, the language must be clear, authoritative, and linguistically natural in **Telugu (`te-IN`)**, **Hindi (`hi-IN`)**, and **English (`en-IN`)**.

Technical jargon must never obscure actionable field instructions (e.g. chemical dilution rates, spray timing, and safety precautions).

## 3. Bilingual Co-Existence of Scientific Names

To ensure scientific precision without confusing farmers:
- In both Telugu and Hindi views, the canonical botanical and pathological names are rendered parenthetically alongside vernacular names (e.g. *"సెర్కోస్పోరా ఆకుమచ్చ (*Cercospora gossypina*)*"*).
- This enables extension officers, university researchers, and farmers to cross-reference prescriptions against state seed and pesticide retail registries.
