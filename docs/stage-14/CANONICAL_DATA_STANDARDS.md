# ACREVIA Stage 14 — Canonical Data Standards & Unit Registry

## 1. Unit Registry & Conversion Factors
The ACREVIA canonical unit registry enforces standard SI units while supporting regional and agricultural trade measures:

| Category | Units Supported | Base Reference | Telangana / Indian Standards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MASS** | `kg`, `gram`, `quintal`, `tonne`, `pound`, `bag_75kg`, `bag_50kg` | 1.0 kg | 1 quintal = 100 kg; 1 paddy bag = 75 kg |
| **AREA** | `ha`, `acre`, `sq_meter`, `gunta` | 1.0 ha | 1 acre = 0.404686 ha; 1 gunta = 1/40th acre (0.010117 ha) |
| **VOLUME** | `liter`, `ml`, `cubic_meter`, `gallon_us` | 1.0 liter | 1 cubic meter = 1,000 liters |
| **CONDUCTIVITY**| `ds_m`, `ms_cm`, `us_cm` | 1.0 dS/m | 1 dS/m = 1 mS/cm = 1,000 uS/cm |
| **CONCENTRATION**| `ppm`, `mg_kg`, `mg_l`, `percent`, `kg_ha` | 1.0 ppm | 1% = 10,000 ppm; macronutrients in kg/ha |
| **RATE** | `kg_ha`, `quintal_acre`, `tonne_ha` | 1.0 kg/ha | 1 quintal/acre = ~247.105 kg/ha |

## 2. Multilingual Crop Taxonomy

### Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*)
- **Botanical Family:** Malvaceae
- **Names:**
  - English: Cotton
  - Telugu: ప్రత్తి (పత్తి)
  - Hindi: कपास
- **Telangana Varieties:** RCH-659 BG II, Mallika BG II, Bhakti BG II, Kaveri Jaadoo, Desi Cotton.
- **Optimal Soil pH:** 6.0 - 8.2 | **Salinity Threshold:** 7.7 dS/m

### Rice / Paddy (*Oryza sativa*)
- **Botanical Family:** Poaceae
- **Names:**
  - English: Rice / Paddy
  - Telugu: వరి
  - Hindi: धान / चावल
- **Telangana Varieties:** BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri), RNR-15048 (Telangana Sona - Low GI), MTU-1010, KNM-118, JGL-18047.
- **Optimal Soil pH:** 5.5 - 7.5 | **Salinity Threshold:** 3.0 dS/m

### Chilli (*Capsicum annuum*)
- **Botanical Family:** Solanaceae
- **Names:**
  - English: Chilli
  - Telugu: మిరప
  - Hindi: मिर्च
- **Telangana Varieties:** Teja (S17 Hot), Guntur Hope (LCA 334), Byadgi, Armoor Local, US-341.
- **Optimal Soil pH:** 6.5 - 7.8 | **Salinity Threshold:** 1.5 dS/m
