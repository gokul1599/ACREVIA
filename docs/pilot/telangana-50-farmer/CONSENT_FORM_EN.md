# Informed Consent Form (English)
## Notice of Consent under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023)
**Project Title:** ACREVIA 50-Farmer Telangana Field Evaluation & Pathology Validation Study  
**Investigators:** ACREVIA Research Group in collaboration with Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Malyal & KVK Kampasagar, Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University (PJTSAU)  
**Target Beneficiaries:** Cotton, Rice, and Chilli Smallholder Farmers in Telangana  

---

### 1. Purpose of the Study
The objective of this research study is to evaluate and refine an artificial intelligence computer vision system for leaf-level pest and disease identification and verify localized agronomic guidance grounded in the PJTSAU Package of Practices under real smallholder farming conditions in Telangana.

---

### 2. Categories of Personal Data Collected
By granting consent, you permit the collection and processing of the following limited data:
1. **Participant Identification:** Name, verified mobile telephone number, village, and mandal.
2. **Farm Profile Data:** Cultivated acreage, active crop variety, sowing/transplanting date, soil classification, and primary irrigation source.
3. **Field Observations:** Smartphone photographs of symptomatic crop leaves, voice/audio field notes, and text descriptions of crop symptoms.
4. **Geolocation Coordinates:** Coarse GPS coordinates (dithered to protect farm privacy) used solely for micro-climate weather forecasting and regional pest risk mapping.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Security Safeguards
- All personal data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) within secure server environments in India.
- Your personal contact information will never be sold, leased, or shared with commercial vendors or marketing organizations.
- In all scientific publications and public reports, your data will be strictly anonymized using synthetic Pilot Participant IDs.

---

### 4. Statutory Rights under DPDP Act 2023 (Section 6 & Section 11)
1. **Right of Withdrawal:** You may withdraw your consent at any time without assigning any reason and without incurring any penalty or loss of benefits.
2. **Right to Erasure:** Upon withdrawal, you have the statutory right to request the permanent deletion of your personal data and uploaded photos from ACREVIA systems.
3. **Right of Access & Correction:** You may inspect the personal data retained about you and request correction of inaccurate entries.
4. **Grievance Redressal:** If you have any privacy concerns, you may contact the designated Data Protection Officer (Email: `dpo@acrevia.app`, Phone: `+91 94400 12345`).
