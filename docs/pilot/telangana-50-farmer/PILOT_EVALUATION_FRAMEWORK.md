# ACREVIA 30-Day Telangana Field Pilot Evaluation Framework
## Scientific, Algorithmic, Usability & Safety Evaluation Methodology
**Target Stage:** Stage 08 Remediation (`TASK-S08-01` / Field Evaluation)  
**Geographic Coverage:** Warangal and Nalgonda Districts, Telangana  
**Cohort Size:** $N = 50$ Smallholder Farmers across 6 Mandals  
**Duration:** 30 Consecutive Days  
**Partners:** Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Malyal & Kampasagar, PJTSAU  

---

## 1. Evaluation Architecture & Four Pillars

The evaluation framework rigorously assesses ACREVIA across four distinct scientific and operational pillars:

```
                            ACREVIA EVALUATION PILLARS
                                        │
     ┌──────────────────┬───────────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────┐
     ▼                  ▼                               ▼                  ▼
[PILLAR 1:          [PILLAR 2:                      [PILLAR 3:         [PILLAR 4:
 SYSTEMS & EDGE]     ML INFERENCE & PATHOLOGY]       KRISHIBRAIN RAG]   HUMAN USABILITY]
 - Sync Integrity    - Top-1/Top-3 Concordance       - PoP Compliance   - SUS Score (>=75)
 - Latency p95       - Calibration Error (ECE)       - Citation Rate    - Voice Adoption
 - Offline Durability- Out-of-Distribution Rejection - Toxic Filter     - Task Completion
```

---

## 2. Pillar 1: Systems & Edge Technical Resilience

Evaluates the offline-first progressive web app and backend infrastructure:
1. **Offline Data Durability:** Percentage of records created without network that are retained in IndexedDB without corruption.
   $$\text{Durability} = \frac{\text{Records Retrieved from IndexedDB on Reconnection}}{\text{Records Generated by Farmer Client}} \times 100\% \quad (\text{Target: } 100.0\%)$$
2. **Synchronization Reliability:** Success rate of queued synchronization requests across cellular connections (2G/3G/4G):
   $$\text{Sync Success Rate} = \frac{\text{Records Successfully Persisted to PostgreSQL}}{\text{Total Queued Synchronization Events}} \times 100\% \quad (\text{Target: } \ge 99.0\%)$$
3. **End-to-End Latency Profile:** Round-trip API latency for synchronous queries (KrishiBrain Q&A, weather forecasts) under field conditions. Target: Median $< 500\text{ ms}$, p95 $< 1500\text{ ms}$.

---

## 3. Pillar 2: Machine Learning & Plant Pathology Ground Truth

Benchmarks the shared MobileNetV3 28-class multi-crop model against independent in-field expert diagnoses:
1. **Top-1 and Top-3 Concordance:**
   - Evaluated on a blinded 20% random sample ($n = 80+$ field observations) independently scored by KVK plant pathologists.
   - Target: Top-1 Concordance $\ge 75\%$; Top-3 Concordance $\ge 85\%$.
2. **Confidence Calibration & Expected Calibration Error (ECE):**
   - Verification that reported confidence reflects true probability:
     $$\text{ECE} = \sum_{m=1}^{M} \frac{|B_m|}{N} \left| \text{acc}(B_m) - \text{conf}(B_m) \right| \quad (\text{Target: ECE } \le 0.12)$$
3. **Out-of-Distribution (OOD) & Image Quality Rejection:**
   - Precision and recall of the image quality filter on non-crop images (dirt, tools, fingers, severe blur).
   - False Rejection Rate target: $< 8.0\%$; False Acceptance Rate target: $< 2.0\%$.

---

## 4. Pillar 3: KrishiBrain RAG & Agronomic Guidance Quality

Assesses the multilingual RAG engine against PJTSAU Package of Practices:
1. **Adherence to PJTSAU PoP:** Blinded review by KVK agronomists of 50 randomly sampled vernacular responses.
   - Scored on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Contradicts PJTSAU; 5 = Perfectly Aligned).
   - Target: Mean score $\ge 4.5 / 5.0$.
2. **Citation Grounding Fidelity:** Percentage of generated responses containing explicit, clickable source citations to university bulletins. Target: $100.0\%$.
3. **Zero Dangerous Chemical Prescriptions:** 0 off-label or banned pesticides recommended without certified agronomist sign-off. Target: **Zero tolerance (0%)**.

---

## 5. Pillar 4: Farmer Usability, Voice Adoption & Behavioral Trust

Measures how smallholder farmers interact with and trust the technology:
1. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** Administered in Telugu to all 50 farmers at Day 30.
   - Target: Cohort Mean Score $\ge 75.0$ (Grade B+ / "Good to Excellent").
2. **Voice Interaction Modality Share:** Ratio of voice-driven queries vs keyboard inputs in KrishiBrain. Target: $\ge 40\%$ voice adoption.
3. **Farmer Task Completion Rate:** Successful independent capture, photo upload, and review without field assistant intervention. Target: $\ge 85\%$.
4. **Self-Reported Agronomic Value:** Farmer satisfaction regarding whether the guidance prevented crop loss or reduced input costs. Target: $\ge 75\%$ positive appraisal.

---

## 6. Pre-Registration & Blinding Controls

To avoid observer bias and Hawthorne effects:
- **Blinded Audits:** KVK pathologists score physical field symptoms on standardized paper datasheets before opening the ACREVIA portal to review AI outputs.
- **Pre-Registered Metrics:** Metric definitions, mathematical numerators, and denominators are frozen before Day 1 of the trial. No post-hoc alteration of success gates is permitted.
- **Confounding Variables:** Weather extremes, device RAM variations, and cellular tower outages are logged daily to separate algorithmic errors from environmental disruptions.
