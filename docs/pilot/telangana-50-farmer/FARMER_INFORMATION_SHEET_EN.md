# Farmer Information Sheet (English)
## ACREVIA Agricultural AI Field Validation Study — Telangana
**Target Region:** Warangal and Nalgonda Districts, Telangana State  
**Institutional Partners:** Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Malyal (Warangal) & KVK Kampasagar (Nalgonda), PJTSAU  
**Target Crops:** Cotton, Rice, Chilli  
**Study Duration:** 30 Calendar Days  

---

### 1. What is ACREVIA?
ACREVIA is a digital agricultural intelligence platform designed specifically for smallholder farmers. Using your Android smartphone, you can photograph symptomatic crop leaves to receive immediate pest and pathology identification, along with management guidance grounded in the official Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University (PJTSAU) Package of Practices.

The application operates seamlessly in offline conditions. Field observations and photos captured without cellular coverage are safely stored on your device and automatically synchronized to the server when network connectivity is restored.

---

### 2. Why is this Field Study Being Conducted?
While computer vision algorithms demonstrate high accuracy in research laboratories, real-world farm conditions involve harsh sunlight, dust, low-cost cameras, and intermittent network connectivity. 

This 30-day field study aims to:
1. Validate AI diagnostic accuracy against direct physical examinations by KVK plant pathologists.
2. Confirm the reliability of offline data storage and background synchronization in rural areas.
3. Assess the clarity, usability, and practical value of Telugu voice and text advisories for farmers.

---

### 3. What Does Your Participation Involve?
If you volunteer to participate:
1. **App Installation:** The village field assistant will install the ACREVIA Progressive Web App (PWA) on your Android phone.
2. **Bi-Weekly Field Scouting:** Over the 30-day period, you will photograph crop leaves showing symptoms at least twice weekly (minimum 8 observations total).
3. **KVK Scientist Interaction:** You will welcome KVK scientists during their routine weekly field visits to examine your plots.
4. **Feedback Sharing:** At the conclusion of Day 30, you will participate in a brief 15-minute exit survey on your experience with the app.

---

### 4. Safety & Agronomist Oversight
- **Chemical Safety Guarantee:** ACREVIA will never automatically recommend dangerous, restricted, or off-label pesticides. Severe or complex disease conditions are placed into an expert review queue, where certified KVK scientists verify the diagnosis before any chemical advice is released.
- **Support:** A dedicated Village Field Assistant and telephone helpline are available daily for any questions or technical issues.

---

### 5. Data Privacy & Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)
- Your data is strictly protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 of India.
- Your personal contact information is encrypted and isolated.
- GPS coordinates are used exclusively for localized weather and crop context, and are dithered to protect farm privacy.
- Your data will never be sold to third-party commercial vendors or advertising networks.
- Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without any negative consequences, and you have the legal right to request the permanent deletion of your data.
