## 3. Agronomic Grounding & Scientific Claim Verification

The scientific claims, regional pest threats, and crop management challenges cited in the collaboration proposals were cross-referenced against published agronomic literature for Telangana:

### 3.1 KVK Malyal Package Review (`KVK_MALYAL_COLLABORATION_PROPOSAL.md`)
- **Focal Crops:** Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*) and Chilli (*Capsicum annuum*).
- **Soils:** Red Sandy Loams (Chalka soils) and Medium Black Soils.
- **Scientific Pathological Context:**
  1. *Cotton Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella):* Well-documented resistance to Cry1Ac and Cry2Ab Bt toxins in Central Telangana; necessitates pheromone trap monitoring, terminal rosette flower inspection, and strict CIBRC chemical rotation.
  2. *Chilli Invasive Black Thrips (Thrips parvispinus):* Severe threat in Telangana chilli belts since 2021; causes flower drop, crinkling, and significant yield collapse. Farmers often resort to unscientific pesticide cocktails. ACREVIA’s computer vision identification and CIBRC safety gate provide timely, non-chemical IPM guidance.
- **Scientific Audit Finding:** The proposal accurately frames ACREVIA as a **decision-support tool** under the double-blind oversight of KVK plant pathologists, avoiding ungrounded yield improvement guarantees.

### 3.2 KVK Kampasagar Package Review (`KVK_KAMPASAGAR_COLLABORATION_PROPOSAL.md`)
- **Focal Crops:** Rice (Paddy - *Oryza sativa*) and Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*).
- **Soils:** Deep Black Vertisols and Heavy Canal Alluvium.
- **Command Area Context:** Nagarjuna Sagar Project (NSP) Left Canal network.
- **Scientific Agronomic Context:**
  1. *Rice Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD):* Crucial technology for water conservation under intermittent canal rotations. ACREVIA’s fusion of Sentinel-2 NDWI moisture monitoring with Open-Meteo evapotranspiration data provides scientific decision support.
  2. *Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae) & Brown Planthopper (Nilaparvata lugens):* Microclimate risk rules (humidity $\ge 90\%$, temperature $20^\circ\text{C}-24^\circ\text{C}$) correlate with blast sporulation, offering 48-hour proactive alerts before visual symptom spread.
- **Scientific Audit Finding:** The proposal separates research hypotheses (potential water conservation and judicious input use) from measured results.

## 5. Non-Binding Collaboration Agreement (MoU) Checklist

Before any formal Memorandum of Understanding is finalized with either institution, the following matters must be aligned:

- [ ] **Institutional Objectives:** Non-exclusive scientific research pilot.
- [ ] **Financial Terms:** 100% free of cost; zero exchange of funds between ACREVIA, KVK, or farmers.
- [ ] **Scope & Geography:** 20–30 farmers in one target village cluster per KVK for 30 days.
- [ ] **Farmer Selection:** KVK scientists facilitate cohort selection based on defined eligibility criteria.
- [ ] **Human-in-the-Loop Oversight:** KVK plant pathologists review and validate automated AI diagnoses via ExpertConnect.
- [ ] **Data Ownership:** Farmers retain 100% ownership of their agronomic data; right to erasure within 48 hours.
- [ ] **Intellectual Property & Publication:** Joint academic authorship on all resulting validation whitepapers.
- [ ] **Liability & CIBRC Compliance:** Clear disclaimers that ACREVIA is a decision-support platform, not a substitute for statutory regulatory approvals.
- [ ] **Termination Clause:** Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice without liability.
