# ACREVIA — Community Trust and Moderation Policy
**Subsystem:** Governance & Moderation  
**Primary Objective:** Maintain platform integrity, scientific grounding, and farmer safety across all collaborative spaces.

---

## 1. Moderation Roles & Permissions

1. **`FARMER`:** Can create questions, share field experiences, comment, and submit knowledge contributions for review.
2. **`VERIFIED_EXPERT`:** Holds credentialed agronomist badge; can submit expert answers, review community contributions, and certify regional signals.
3. **`RESEARCHER`:** Can lead research projects, create annotation tasks, and export approved datasets.
4. **`ORGANIZATION_REPRESENTATIVE`:** Manages verified collective/FPO workspaces and official extension events.
5. **`COMMUNITY_MODERATOR`:** Triages flagged posts, moderates spam, and enforces community rules.
6. **`ADMIN`:** Full platform governance, role assignment, and organization approval.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content Categories

- **Dangerous Chemical Recommendations:** Promoting banned pesticides, off-label chemical applications, or toxic concoctions that violate Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) directives.
- **Fabricated Scientific Citations:** Generating fake research papers or attributing claims falsely to universities or research institutes.
- **Aggressive Commercial Solicitation:** Unsolicited marketing by private input dealers disguised as neutral farmer advice.
- **Sensational Outbreak Panic:** Spreading unverified claims of agricultural epidemics or severe regional crop failure without diagnostic backing.
- **Impersonation & False Credentials:** Claiming agronomist, researcher, or government official status without verified platform credentials.

---

## 3. Transparency & Appeals Workflow

- **Moderation Notice:** When content is hidden or removed, the author receives an explanatory notice detailing the violated policy category.
- **Appeal Mechanism:** Authors can lodge a formal appeal (`AppealRecord`) requesting human review by a chief moderator or agronomist.
- **Audit Logging:** Every moderation action (hide, remove, reinstate, ban) is recorded in immutable audit logs with author, timestamp, and justification.
